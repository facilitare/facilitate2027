# Cvent capability questions

Two messages, ready to send. The first is for Jocelyn, who owns the platform; the second
is the short framing for Helene, who owns the process decision.

The purpose is not to argue for a separate tool. It is to close an open question cheaply:
the selection process has three requirements that a generic reviewer module may or may not
support, and nobody on the team has used the module yet.

---

## Message 1 — to Jocelyn (platform capability)

> Subject: Cvent — a few questions about the session review stage for FACILITATE 2027
>
> Hi Jocelyn,
>
> We're setting up the session selection process for the Europe Region conference in 2027,
> and we'd like to build it around Cvent rather than around a spreadsheet if the platform
> can support it.
>
> The selection policy commits us to a few specific things — anonymous first-round
> assessment, a fixed 0/1/2 scoring rubric, and a set of diversity and track targets for
> the final programme. Before we commit the process to any tool, could you tell us what
> Cvent can and can't do on the points below? Most should be a yes or a no, and we're just
> as glad to hear a no — it tells us where we need a workaround.
>
> **Access and licensing**
>
> 1. Our assessment team is around six people. Does each reviewer need a full Cvent login,
>    or is there a reviewer role that doesn't consume one of our licences?
> 2. If reviewers do need logins, how many do we have available in total, and what would it
>    cost to add more?
>
> **Anonymous first-round review**
>
> 3. Can a submission be shown to reviewers with the applicant's identity hidden, while an
>    administrator still sees the full record? Is that controlled field by field, or is it
>    all-or-nothing?
> 4. Applicants routinely name themselves and their company inside free-text answers. Can
>    an administrator edit or redact a submitted answer before reviewers see it, while
>    keeping the original on file?
> 5. Can we release the personal-characteristics questions (region, career stage, age band,
>    gender, ethnicity) only at a second stage, after scoring is complete?
>
> **Independent scoring**
>
> 6. Can a reviewer be prevented from seeing other reviewers' scores and comments until
>    they have submitted their own? This one matters most to us — it's the reason we're
>    moving off a shared spreadsheet.
> 7. Can we assign a specific subset of reviewers to each submission, rather than every
>    reviewer seeing everything?
>
> **The scoring form**
>
> 8. Can a scoring criterion use a 0/1/2 scale where each of the three points carries its
>    own written description, shown to the reviewer on the scoring screen itself rather
>    than in a separate guidance document?
> 9. Can one score cover several application questions read together? Our panel scores four
>    criteria holistically, not question by question.
>
> **After the scores**
>
> 10. Does Cvent flag where reviewers disagree, or would we spot that by reading a report?
> 11. Can it report the selected set against our own targets — for example the share of
>     session hosts based outside England and Wales, the share under 35, and the balance
>     across our four conference tracks — and update as we add or remove sessions?
> 12. Can it assemble the written feedback from several reviewers into a single message per
>     applicant?
>
> Happy to walk through these in Christine's onboarding session if that's easier than
> answering in writing.
>
> Thanks,
> Bogdan

## Message 2 — to Helene (why it's worth asking)

> Hi Helene,
>
> Understood on holding off on the app — no argument from me, and if Cvent covers this
> it's much better that it lives where the rest of the conference lives.
>
> Before we assume it does, I've written out twelve questions for Jocelyn, since nobody on
> our side has used that part of Cvent yet. Three of them would settle it either way:
>
> **Licences.** We have up to five Cvent logins and an assessment team of about six. If
> reviewers consume a licence, the platform can't host the process as we're currently
> staffed — and that's a five-minute email to find out.
>
> **Blind review.** The policy commits us to assessing proposals without reference to the
> applicant's identity. That needs the platform to hide identity fields from reviewers, and
> it needs someone able to redact the applicant's name where they've written it into their
> own answers. If Cvent can't do both, our anonymity is a matter of everyone remembering to
> look away.
>
> **Independent scoring.** In a shared sheet, the third assessor to open it has already
> seen two scores. Whether Cvent can withhold colleagues' scores until you've submitted
> your own is the single thing that most affects the quality of our ranking.
>
> If all three come back yes, I'll drop it happily and we build the process in Cvent.
>
> I've also put together a short clickable mock-up of the three screens I had in mind, so
> the questions are concrete rather than theoretical — happy to show it in ten minutes at
> the next call, or not at all if it's a distraction at this stage.
>
> Bogdan
